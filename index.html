How it works

  1. Do your set

    Log the weight and reps with a tap, or just say them out loud.

  2. Say what was left

    Zero to five-plus reps left in the tank. An honest guess is all it needs.

  3. Get your next set

    Heavier, lighter or the same. Your coach explains why, and you approve every change.

What does "reps left in the tank" mean?

How many more reps you could have done with good form before you had to stop. Two left means the set was hard but you had a little more. Your coach uses that number to pick your next weight.

Do I need a gym?

No. There are plans for a full gym, a few dumbbells at home, or just your bodyweight.

Is Gain Gauge free?

Yes, to start. Workouts, your coach, food search, recovery check-ins and the progress photo gallery are free. Gain Gauge Pro adds hands-free voice logging, barcode scanning, smart coach chat, long-term progress and progress photo tools. Prices will be shown in the App Store at launch.

Who are Everett and Iona?

They're AI-generated coach characters, not real people. Their voices come from your iPhone, so nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.

Is my data private?

Yes. There's no account and no server. Your workouts, food, check-ins and photos stay on your iPhone, and we never see them. Read the privacy policy.

What do I need to use it?

An iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Apple Watch is optional. Smart coach chat needs an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence.

Is this medical advice?

No. Gain Gauge gives general fitness information. Check with your doctor before starting a new program, and stop if you feel pain, dizziness or chest discomfort.
