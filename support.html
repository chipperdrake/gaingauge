Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There's no account, so nothing syncs between phones. Use Settings › Export to save a copy.

How do I cancel Gain Gauge Pro? On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Gain Gauge. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

I bought Pro but it isn't showing. Open the app's Settings › Gain Gauge Pro › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The coach can't hear me. Check Settings › Gain Gauge › Microphone and Speech Recognition are on.

A food or barcode is missing. Tap "Add food" and type the nutrition from the label. The food list grows with app updates.

Safety

Gain Gauge gives general fitness information, not medical advice. Stop and get help if you feel chest pain, dizziness, faintness, shortness of breath or sharp pain. In the U.S., call or text 988 if you're in crisis.
