Accessibility

Updated November 21, 2026 · Gain Gauge is made by Spud Labs LLC, Meridian, Idaho

Our goal: everyone should be able to use Gain Gauge and this website. We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, level AA, and we fix problems people report to us.

In the app

  • VoiceOver: buttons and controls have spoken labels, and coach portraits are described as AI-generated characters.
  • Larger Text: app text follows the text size you set in your iPhone's Settings.
  • Reduce Motion: when it is on in your iPhone's Settings, the app tones down its animations.
  • Coach voice: everything the coach says aloud is also shown as text on screen, and you can turn the voice off.
  • Talking or typing: you can type to your coach any time instead of speaking.

On this website

  • Pages work with screen readers and keyboard navigation.
  • Images have text descriptions.
  • Text and buttons have strong color contrast, and links and buttons are large enough to tap easily.
  • Pages work when zoomed in and on small phone screens.

Known limits

Shareable workout and progress images are pictures with fixed text sizes. The same information is available as regular text in the app. Gain Gauge is still in development, and we are testing it with VoiceOver and large text sizes before launch.

Report a problem

If something in the app or on this website is hard to use with your setup, please tell us. Include what you were trying to do and the assistive technology you use, if any.

Email support@getspudlabs.com. We will reply and work on a fix or another way to get you what you need.
