GAIN GAUGE

Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

The short version: Gain Gauge does not collect, sell or share your personal data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores on your iPhone

Your workouts, food entries, recovery check-ins, body weight, coach chat, settings and the coach you picked. This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device, protected by your iPhone's passcode encryption, and excluded from iCloud backups.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing. Gain Gauge has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

  • Coach voice: spoken by Apple's built-in voices, on your device.
  • Talking to your coach: speech is turned into text on your iPhone only, and only while the microphone button is active.
  • Smart coach replies (Pro): generated by Apple's on-device model on supported iPhones. Your questions stay on your phone.
  • Barcode scanning (Pro): the camera image is read on your iPhone and is never saved or sent. The food list is built into the app, so lookups never leave your phone.
  • Purchases: if you buy Gain Gauge Pro, Apple handles the payment. We never see your card details or your Apple ID.
  • Apple Health (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, finished workouts and body weight are saved to the Health app on your iPhone. Gain Gauge does not read your Health data, and never shares, sells or uses Health data for advertising, and never stores it in iCloud.
  • Sharing: the workout image is created on your phone and only goes where you choose to share it.

Permissions

Microphone and speech recognition (only when you tap to talk or turn on hands-free mode), camera (only when you scan a barcode), and Apple Health (only if you turn on Health sync). You can turn these off anytime in your iPhone's Settings.

Your choices

Export everything as spreadsheet files, or delete everything, anytime in the app's Settings. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Health information

Gain Gauge gives general fitness information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything. Food data comes from USDA FoodData Central (public domain); nutrition values are estimates.

Children

Gain Gauge is intended for adults 18 and older and is not directed to children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above, and the app will show the new version.

Contact

Questions about privacy: gaingaugeapp@gmail.com or the support page.
